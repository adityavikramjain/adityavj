Defining the Landscape

Augmented Reality (AR)

  • Overlaying digital information on the physical world
  • Primary Device: Smartphone (High Accessibility)
  • Core Utility: Contextual Information / Visualization

Virtual Reality (VR)

  • Replacing the physical world with a simulated environment
  • Primary Device: Headset (High Immersion)
  • Core Utility: Training / Simulation / Remote Presence

The Core Challenge: The Visualization Gap

The Mental Model

In digital channels, there is often a gap between the 'Asset' (image/video) and the 'Context' (where the product will live).

AR is one method to close this gap.

The 'Why': AR Outperforms Static Media

Attention Span

11x vs Traditional Social

Purchase Intent

"In what other business can you fail to engage 95% of your audience and be considered a success?" — Ferenci

The Friction: Why Everyone Wasn't Doing It

Traditional Agency Model

  • Cost: $5,000 - $50,000 per experience
  • Time: Weeks of development
  • Scalability: Zero. (Different code for Snap, FB, IG)

The Camera IQ Solution

  • No Code: Drag-and-drop design tools.
  • Scalable: 'Create once, deploy everywhere'.

The Strategic Dilemma

Where do we allocate limited resources?

1. Go Broad:
SMBs (High Volume)

2. Go Deep:
Enterprise (High Value)

Analyzing the Unit Economics

SMB Segment

CAC ~$15,000
Churn 4.3% / mo

Sales Cycle: Fast

Enterprise Segment

CAC ~$40,000
Churn 1.6% / mo

Sales Cycle: Slow

Key Insight
  • Volume vs. Value: 100k Potential SMBs vs 5k Enterprises.
  • Retention Risk: SMB churn is nearly 3x higher than Enterprise.

Discussion: The Case for SMBs

Discussion Prompt

Why might a company choose the SMB path despite high churn?

Factors to consider: Market speed, learning loops, cash flow velocity.

Discussion: The Case for Enterprise

Discussion Prompt

Why might the Enterprise path be safer despite the high CAC?

Factors to consider: Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), Credibility, Integration depth.

The Impact of Churn on Valuation

Retention drives Long-Term Value.

< 2 Years Average SMB Life (4.3% Churn)
> 5 Years Average Enterprise Life (1.6% Churn)

Framework: Choosing Your Lane

SMB Strategy Fits When...

  • Product needs rapid iteration
  • Market education is low
  • Solution is 'Plug and Play'

Enterprise Strategy Fits When...

  • Integration is complex
  • Brand safety is paramount
  • Reliability > Novelty
